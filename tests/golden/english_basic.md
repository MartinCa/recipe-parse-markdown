Light, fluffy pancakes for a lazy Sunday morning.

## Ingredients

- 300 g plain flour
- 2 eggs
- 450 ml milk
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp sugar

## Directions

1. Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
2. Beat the eggs and milk together, then stir into the dry mix.
3. Cook spoonfuls of batter in a hot, lightly oiled pan until golden.
