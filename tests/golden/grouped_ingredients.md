## Ingredients

**Cake**
- 300 g flour
- 400 g sugar
- 100 g cocoa powder

**Frosting**
- 200 g butter
- 300 g icing sugar
- 50 g cocoa powder

## Directions

1. Bake the two sponge layers and let them cool completely.
2. Whip the frosting until smooth, then stack and frost the layers.
