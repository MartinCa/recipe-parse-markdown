## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 lb butter, softened
- 8 oz chocolate chips
- 1 tsp baking soda

## Directions

1. Cream the butter and sugars together.
2. Mix in the dry ingredients and chocolate chips.
3. Bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes.
