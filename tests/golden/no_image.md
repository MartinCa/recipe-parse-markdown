## Ingredients

- 1 kg ripe tomatoes
- 1 onion, chopped
- 500 ml vegetable stock

## Directions

1. Saute the onion until soft.
2. Add the tomatoes and stock, then simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Blend until smooth.
